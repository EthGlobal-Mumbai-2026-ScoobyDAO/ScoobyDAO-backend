# Legacy mathematical method reference, v1
This describes the recovered implementation; use the values in the supplied report as authoritative. The legacy implementation trains a VAE on the supplied daily return series and generates 50 reconstruction columns. Its Student-t reparameterization uses 2.2 degrees of freedom. Equal weights of 1/50 are used across reconstruction columns in the covariance calculation. VaR uses the normal quantile times the square root of the weighted covariance and square root of the stated horizon, then multiplies by 100. Expected shortfall is calculated separately from the lower 5% daily tail for each reconstruction and averaged; it is not scaled to the VaR horizon. The output field named max_drawdown is the minimum single reconstructed return, not conventional peak-to-trough drawdown. Daily and annualized volatility, score, risk labels and technical indicators retain their original definitions. The original run has no fixed random seed; reconstructions are in-sample and their independence, calibration, and transfer from stocks to tokens have not been established. Missing VAE output is pending; do not create or substitute values. Never claim this model proves safety or identifies a honeypot.
